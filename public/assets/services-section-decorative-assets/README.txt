Recommended placement
---------------------
- Position decorative SVGs absolutely inside each panel.
- Keep them behind all text/content with pointer-events: none.
- Crop with overflow: hidden.
- Use opacity in the 0.08–0.22 range depending on the panel.
- Keep BUILD on Warm Bone.
- Use progressively deeper Forest shades for AUTOMATE, SECURE, SCALE.
- Keep live headings, service names, descriptions, buttons and list icons as HTML/SVG — not baked into images.
